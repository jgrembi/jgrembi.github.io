Today, Nazifa shared her work at the Life Sciences Symposium!  While it's always exciting to share our work and receive feedback, I'm extra proud of her accomplishments in just 5 months since joining the lab.  

Her poster, *"Associations Between Maternal Iron Status and the Gut Microbiome in a South Asian Pregnancy Cohort"*, highlighted several analyses exploring how maternal microbiome composition relates to iron deficiency during pregnancy.
