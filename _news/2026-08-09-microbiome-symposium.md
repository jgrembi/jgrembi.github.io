The Grembi Lab was well represented at this year's biennial One Health Microbiome Symposium. 

![Grembi Lab group photo!](/images/microbiome-symposium.jpg)

Research technologists Eliza Daigle and Pablo "Eddie" Ochoa-Anderson presented posters, alongside our collaborator and UC Santa Cruz PhD student Md Ziaur Rahman, who is co-advised by Dr. Grembi. Graduate student Nazifa Tabassum served on the symposium's organizing committee.
